How it works

The End-to-End Lifecycle

How recommendations flow securely from initial suggestion to final payment delivery.

01

Recommend

A grant is recommended via the donor portal, advisor interface, or API integration.

02

Approve

The recommendation is approved within your firm's compliance controls & workflows.

03

Validate

FlightPath automatically verifies the charity's registration and onboarding details.

04

Execute

The digital payment is dispatched securely to the charity via optimized paths.

05

Track

The transaction is reconciled and tracked to completion with complete audit visibility.
